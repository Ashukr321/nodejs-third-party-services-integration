# Use Cases - Maps & Location Services 🗺️

Location-based services add physical context to your digital application.

### 1. Address Autocomplete
Helping users fill in addresses quickly and accurately during checkout or signup.

### 2. Delivery & Fleet Tracking
Real-time tracking of drivers, packages, or vehicles.
- **Example:** Uber, DoorDash.

### 3. Geofencing
Triggering actions when a user enters or leaves a specific geographic area.
- **Example:** Sending a discount coupon when a user walks near a store.

### 4. Nearby Search (Points of Interest)
Finding the closest ATM, restaurant, or hospital based on the user's coordinates.

### 5. Distance & Routing
Calculating travel time and optimized routes between multiple points.
- **Example:** Logistics planning.
