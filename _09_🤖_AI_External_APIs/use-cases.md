# Use Cases - AI & External APIs 🤖

AI integrations bring intelligence and automation to your application.

### 1. Intelligent Chatbots
Providing 24/7 customer support, answering FAQs, or guiding users through complex tasks.

### 2. Content Generation & Summarization
Automatically writing blog posts, summarizing long documents, or generating product descriptions.

### 3. Sentiment Analysis
Analyzing user reviews or social media mentions to understand the emotional tone (Positive, Negative, Neutral).

### 4. Image & Speech Recognition
Identifying objects in photos, transcribing audio to text, or translating languages in real-time.

### 5. Recommendation Engines
Suggesting products, articles, or movies based on user preferences and history.
- **Example:** Netflix "Top Picks", Amazon "Customers also bought".
