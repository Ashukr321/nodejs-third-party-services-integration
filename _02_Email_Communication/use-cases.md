# Use Cases - Email & Communication 📩

Email is the backbone of user communication and notification systems.

### 1. Transactional Emails
Sent automatically based on a user's action.
- **Examples:** Password reset links, account verification (OTP), order confirmations, invoices.

### 2. Marketing & Newsletters
Sent to a list of subscribers to promote products or share content.
- **Examples:** Weekly digests, promotional offers, product updates.

### 3. System Alerts
Notifying administrators or users about system-level events.
- **Examples:** Low balance alerts, security breach notifications, server downtime.

### 4. Drip Campaigns (Automation)
A series of pre-written emails sent over time.
- **Examples:** Onboarding sequence for new users (Day 1, Day 3, Day 7).
