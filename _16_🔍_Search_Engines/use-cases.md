# Use Cases - Search Engines 🔍

Providing fast, relevant, and complex search capabilities.

### 1. E-commerce Product Search
Searching through thousands of products with filters for category, price, color, and size.

### 2. Full-Text Search in Blogs/Articles
Finding relevant content based on keywords, even if the exact phrase isn't present (fuzzy search).

### 3. Autocomplete & Suggestions
Predicting what a user is typing in real-time.

### 4. Geo-Search
Finding "restaurants within 5 miles of me" with high performance.

### 5. Log Analysis
Searching through terabytes of log data for specific error patterns (e.g., Elasticsearch).
